Concept & Planning
This project started from my childhood dream of having a personal stylist at my fingertips. I envisioned an app that would make getting dressed fun, easy, and creative. My goal is to design a digital wardrobe where users can organize their clothes and get daily outfit suggestions that fit their style.

Research & Inspiration
I explored several similar apps and GitHub projects to see what features worked well. I looked at how they handled wardrobe organization, outfit recommendations, and how they integrated machine learning. This helped me refine what I wanted in my app and how I could make it unique.

Building the Foundation
The next step is to set up the core functionality:

A virtual closet where users can upload and categorize their clothes

A clean, simple interface to manage wardrobe items

Basic outfit suggestion logic based on weather, occasion, and style preferences

Smart Styling Features
I’ll integrate AI-powered suggestions to help users create outfits with their existing wardrobe. I want to add mix-and-match capabilities where users can experiment with different combinations before trying things on. There will also be a planner to schedule outfits for future events.

Shopping Integration & Additional Features
I plan to enhance the app by suggesting pieces that complement the user’s wardrobe, helping them make thoughtful fashion purchases. I’ll also include outfit reminders and special features that allow users to plan for occasions ahead of time.
